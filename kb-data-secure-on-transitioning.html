Data Secure on Transitioning to a New Office

Home / Knowledge Base / Data Secure on Transition...

It is far wiser to do your own installations and ensure that all data flowing through the organization is fully secured during a physical or structural transition.

Cloud Migration Strategies

When transitioning to a new office or upgrading your legacy infrastructure, moving to a cloud model (Azure, AWS, or Hybrid) can eliminate the risks of physical data transit. The process begins by auditing all existing workloads, mapping dependencies, and calculating Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) to ensure the cloud model is cost-effective.

Achieving Zero Downtime

Execution of a data transition should occur during non-business hours to achieve zero downtime. Utilizing enterprise-grade tools like Azure Site Recovery and real-time database mirroring ensures that while physical servers are being relocated or decommissioned, the virtual equivalents are immediately spun up and serving requests without interruption.

Compliance and Data Sovereignty

During transitions, compliance cannot be an afterthought. Choosing between PaaS, IaaS, and SaaS models heavily depends on regulatory compliance, specifically considering GDPR, ISO 27001 constraints, and data residency laws. Ensuring data is encrypted both at rest and in transit is non-negotiable for enterprise deployments.
